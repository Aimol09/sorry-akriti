Did you mess up? Let’s fix it with Love. ❤️

Gusse ko pyar mein badalne ke liye perfect 'Sorry' messages.

Chhoti Galtiyon Ke Liye 🌸

"I am really sorry, babu. Mera irada tumhe hurt karne ka bilkul nahi tha. Please maaf kar do?"

"I hate it when we fight. Main apni galti maanta hoon. I love you, please smile now!"

Jab Dil Zyada Dukhaya Ho 💔

"Main janta hoon meri wajah se tumhara dil dukha hai. I promise I'll try my best to never hurt you like this again. I am truly sorry."

"Tum meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ho. Meri duniya tumhare bina adhuri hai. Please mujhe ek aakhri mauka de do."

Cute & Funny (Manane Ke Liye) 🧸

"I am sorry! Main ek number ka idiot hoon, but yaad rakhna... main *tumhara* idiot hoon. Maaf kar do na?"

"Mera dimag kabhi-kabhi Wi-Fi ki tarah disconnect ho jata hai. Par mera pyar tumhare liye lifetime unlimited plan hai. Sorry!"

A sincere apology has three parts: I am sorry, It was my fault, and How do I make it right? ❤️
